Engine examples

  • static: many meshes with distinct materials, sun shadows, fog. ?n= sets the count.
  • three: the same scene drawn by three, for comparison. ?webgpu uses its WebGPURenderer.
  • materials: textures, a normal map, water as a ShaderMaterial, blending, a decal.
  • particles: GPU-simulated explosions and smoke. ?stress=100000 keeps that many alive.
  • models: the game's GLB models, 64 skinned players blending clips.
